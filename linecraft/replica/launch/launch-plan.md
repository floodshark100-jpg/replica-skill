# Launch plan

Before launch
- Connect a licensed data feed, wire auth and Stripe (`../backend.md`). Nothing below happens on fictional data.
- Run the name checks in `../brand.md`; talk to a lawyer about the name and about gambling-adjacent marketing in your states/countries.
- Waitlist on the landing page; analytics (privacy-friendly, no cookie banner) and error tracking live.
- Collect 100+ real reviews of prop tools in a browser and rerun `reviews.py` before you lock the angle.

Where the users are
- Prop-betting and DFS pick'em subreddits and Discords: lead with the "rating that shows its work", post a real prop breakdown, not an ad. Read each community's self-promo rules first.
- Product Hunt: lead with the fix (transparent rating + works on any phone).

First 10 users
- Hand-pick bettors who post their own research. Give them Pro free for a month, watch them use the board, ask what they'd pay.

Store (later, with Expo): `listing.json` passes `listing.py`. Apple 4.1 (copycats): our own name, palette, mark and the visible rating breakdown are what distinguish it; show the breakdown in screenshot 1.
